Membership benefits

What membership offers, with activities created by members for members.

The best way to experience the Mensa Korea community is to take part. If you belong to another national Mensa and plan to visit Korea, contact the International Affairs Committee at iac@mensakorea.org before your trip.

Meet as equals

Mensa gives you the chance to meet people you might not encounter otherwise and to engage with them as equals. Members range from high school students to retirees, all connected by the same qualifying standard.

Events all year

Members organize monthly gatherings, regional meetups, new-member orientations, and special events throughout the year. Dates are posted on the notice board and shared calendar.

The Mensa Korea Journal

Distributed to active members, the journal features news, member essays, event recaps, and puzzles. The views expressed by contributors are their own.

Read the latest issue

Special Interest Groups

Special Interest Groups, or SIGs, bring members together around a wide range of interests. If no existing group matches yours, you can gather a few members and start one. For many members, SIGs are the heart of the community.

Committees

Volunteer committees manage the journal, events, international affairs, and public outreach, including environmental cleanup campaigns. Serving on a committee is a good way to meet other members and contribute to the organization.

SIGHT

This international network connects travelers with Mensans in the countries they visit and gives local members opportunities to host visitors. A coordinator supports both guests and hosts.

How SIGHT works

Mensa Korea Academy and store

Members have access to the Academy, a program of courses taught largely by members, and to the Mensa Korea store for membership cards, certificates, and merchandise.

Keeping your membership

Membership requires annual dues, which can be renewed on the Korean site. Your qualifying status does not expire, so you can reactivate a lapsed membership without taking the admission test again.

Renew your membership

Not a member yet?

Start by earning a qualifying score. Then submit a membership application and pay the one-time admission fee and annual dues.

Apply for the test

Questions in English

The International Affairs Committee handles inquiries from members abroad and non-Korean-speaking members in Korea.
