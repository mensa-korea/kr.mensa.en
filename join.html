How to join

One supervised test, completed in about an hour, with no subject-specific study required.

The admission test

For new applicants testing in Korea, eligibility for Mensa Korea membership begins with a qualifying score on the Mensa Korea Admission Test (MKAT). The test assesses nonverbal reasoning through figures and patterns. It requires no knowledge of a particular language and no math. Qualified psychologists oversee its administration and scoring.

To qualify, you need a score at or above the 98th percentile, meaning your score must be higher than that of 98% of the general population. This corresponds to approximately IQ 148 on the SD 24 scale used here. No subject-specific study is required.

Admission test at a glance
EligibilityAge 14 or older on the application date
LengthAbout one hour including instructions
FormatFigural reasoning only — no language, no arithmetic
Qualifying score98th percentile or above
ResultsPublished within four weeks on the test site
WhereSeoul, Busan, Daegu, Daejeon, and Gwangju
RetakesAllowed once a year if you have not passed; no lifetime limit

Step by step

  1. Create an account and apply

    Register at test.mensakorea.kr, open the test schedule, choose a date and a session, and upload a passport-style photo. The photo is used only on your admission ticket.

  2. Print your admission ticket

    Once payment is confirmed, your registration will appear on the site and you can print your admission ticket. Screenshots and digital copies are not accepted; bring the printed ticket.

  3. Take the test

    Doors open 30 minutes before the session for ID checks and seating, and close five minutes before the start. Pens are provided at the venue. Phones must be turned off, and watches of any kind are not allowed.

  4. Check your result

    Results appear in your account within four weeks. If you qualify, you will be invited to apply for full membership and to attend an orientation session.

What to bring

  • Your printed admission ticket.
  • Photo ID showing your date of birth: passport, Residence Card, Korean national ID card, driver's license, or mobile ID.
  • Middle and high school students may instead bring a student ID, proof of enrollment, or youth ID.

ID without a photograph or a date of birth is not accepted, and candidates who cannot be identified will not be admitted. No refund is given in that case.

Cancellations and refunds

You may cancel through the application details page and receive a full refund until registration closes. No refunds are available after the deadline. A no-show does not count as a test attempt.

Retaking the test

The former lifetime limit of three attempts was removed in June 2026. If you have not qualified, you may retake the test once 12 months have passed since your previous attempt.

After you pass

After earning a qualifying score, you may submit a membership application and pay the one-time admission fee and annual dues. New members are invited to orientation sessions held throughout the year in Seoul, Busan, Daegu, and Gwangju, where committees and SIGs introduce their activities.

The online challenge test

Mensa Korea also offers the official Mensa Brain Challenge online. It is designed for entertainment and provides a preview of the question format, but it does not qualify you for membership. For new applicants testing in Korea, the supervised MKAT is the qualifying test.

Test materials are confidential

Test items are the intellectual property of Mensa International. Test booklets and answer sheets may not be removed from the venue or shared, and staff cannot discuss items, answers, or pass rates with candidates. This rule applies across all national Mensa organizations.

Applying without Korean

The registration site is currently available only in Korean, but the test itself is nonverbal. If you need help registering, email support@mensa.kr.

Already qualified elsewhere?

If you belong to another national Mensa and are moving to Korea, contact the International Affairs Committee at iac@mensakorea.org to confirm the applicable transfer process.
