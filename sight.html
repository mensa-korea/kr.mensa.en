SIGHT

The Service of Information, Guidance and Hospitality to Travellers.

What SIGHT is

SIGHT is Mensa's international network for information, guidance, and hospitality. It connects traveling Mensans with local members who are willing to share advice, meet in person, or host a visitor. More than 50 countries offer the service.

Why it exists

A SIGHT coordinator helps visitors and hosts connect and gives both sides the information they need to make suitable arrangements.

Some guests request short-term lodging. More often, they want to meet local members for a meal or coffee, get practical advice, or explore a place with someone who knows it well.

Visiting Korea as a guest

If you belong to another national Mensa and plan to visit Korea, contact the Mensa Korea SIGHT coordinator well before your trip. You may also ask the International Affairs Committee about gatherings scheduled during your stay.

When you contact the coordinator, describe your request and include your itinerary along with:

  • Your name, mobile number, and email address
  • The languages you speak, your Mensa membership number, and your age
  • Any special requests or interests
  • The number of people in your party

The coordinator will contact potential hosts. If a match is found, the coordinator will share the host's contact information so you can make arrangements directly. Tell the coordinator immediately if a problem arises.

What SIGHT is not

SIGHT supports leisure travel and tourism for Mensans. It does not provide immigration assistance, employment placement, or long-term housing.

Hosting as a Mensa Korea member

Hosting can be as simple as meeting a visitor for coffee near your office. If you would like to join the host list, tell the coordinator which city you live in, what you can offer, and which languages you speak.
