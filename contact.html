Contact us

Staff work from the Seoul office on weekdays. Email is usually the fastest way to reach us.

Who to write to

  • support@mensa.kr Admission test, registration, results, and general inquiries.
  • gansa@mensakorea.org Assistant Administrator — membership and office matters.
  • sight@mensakorea.org SIGHT Coordinator — hospitality for traveling Mensans.
  • iac@mensakorea.org International Affairs Committee — visiting members and inquiries in English.
  • +82 2 6341 3177 Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. KST (UTC+9). The phone line may not be staffed at all times.

Where we are

The office and the Seoul test venue are in Gangnam. The office is closed on Korean public holidays.
