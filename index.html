TOP 2%THE HIGH IQ SOCIETY

Where intelligence creates new connections

Mensa began in Oxford in 1946 and now has about 150,000 members in more than 100 countries. Mensa Korea is its national organization in the Republic of Korea, with about 3,000 members nationwide. Applicants must be at least 14 years old, but education, occupation, and nationality do not affect the qualifying standard.

ABOUTSINCE 1946

A community built on one standard and equal standing

Mensa was founded in Oxford in 1946 by Roland Berrill, a barrister and Lancelot Ware, a scientist and lawyer. They envisioned a society whose only membership criterion was measured intelligence, and where race, religion, politics, education, and social background would be irrelevant.

Identify and foster intelligence

Mensa exists to find human intelligence wherever it occurs and to put it to use for the benefit of humanity.

Encourage research into it

The society supports research into the nature, characteristics and uses of intelligence.

Create an engaging community

Members find an intellectually stimulating social environment where they can exchange ideas, pursue shared interests, and build lasting connections.

COMMUNITYSIGS AND COMMITTEES

What members actually do

A qualifying score makes you eligible to apply for membership. Once admitted, members shape nearly every part of the community themselves.

Special Interest Groups

Dozens of member-run SIGs, from board games and hiking to philosophy and programming. If nothing fits, start one.

Gatherings and orientations

Regular meetups in Seoul, Busan, Daegu, Daejeon, and Gwangju, plus orientation sessions for new members.

Committees

Volunteer committees manage the journal, events, international affairs, and outreach activities that include annual environmental cleanups.

ADMISSIONMKAT

The admission test in three steps

No subject-specific study is required. Register, take the test, and wait for your result.

  1. Register and pay

    Create an account on the test site, choose a date and session, upload a photo for your admission ticket, and pay the KRW 88,000 fee. A seat will be reserved when the order payment is successfully completed.

  2. Take the test

    Bring a printed admission ticket and photo ID. The session takes about an hour including instructions and consists entirely of figures and patterns.

  3. Get your result

    Results will be published on the test site within four weeks. A score in the top 2% makes you eligible to apply for membership.

Applying from abroad, or without Korean?

The registration site is currently available only in Korean, but the test itself is nonverbal. If you need help for registration, email support@mensa.kr.

UPDATES2026

What has changed recently

Several things worth knowing if your last glance at Mensa Korea happened a while ago.

  1. July 2026

    New Korean website at mensa.kr

    The Korean site has moved to mensa.kr. Member community, notices, journal and membership renewal are available there.

  2. July 2026

    An online challenge test opened

    The Mensa Brain Challenge is Mensa Korea's official online challenge for entertainment and a preview of the question format. It does not qualify you for membership. For new applicants testing in Korea, the supervised Mensa Korea Admission Test is the qualifying test.

  3. June 2026

    The lifetime limit of three attempts was dropped

    Candidates who have not yet passed may now retake the test as often as they wish, so long as a year has passed since their last attempt.

  4. February 2026

    Testing for under-18s resumed

    Applicants who are at least 14 years old on the application date may take the admission test. Middle and high school students may use a student ID, proof of enrollment, or youth ID as photo identification.

  5. January 2026

    Mensa Korea will host the 2027 IBD

    The International Board of Directors, the highest decision-making body of Mensa International, will meet in Korea in 2027.
